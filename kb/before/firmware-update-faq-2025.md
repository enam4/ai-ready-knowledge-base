# Firmware Update FAQ (2025)

<!-- issue:1007 stale -->If a player shows error 104 or a black screen after a firmware update, wait 5 minutes and then factory reset the player from the settings menu. The player will download its content again after the reset.

<!-- issue:1008 stale -->If the player loses Wi-Fi after an update, connect a USB keyboard to the player and re-enter the Wi-Fi password in the network settings.

<!-- issue:none -->This page applies to firmware 3.x.
