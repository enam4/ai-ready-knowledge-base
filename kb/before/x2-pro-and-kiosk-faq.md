# X2 Pro and K1 Kiosk FAQ

<!-- issue:1009 -->**Q: The X2 Pro keeps restarting?**
A: Almost always heat. They get installed in closed cabinets or right behind the screen. Look at the event log for restarts, get the site to give it some space around the vents. Error E-310 is the thermal one.

<!-- issue:1010 -->**Q: Content won't download on the X2 Pro?**
A: Check storage in the console. Old playlists pile up on these, delete the ones that aren't scheduled anymore and sync again. Usually shows as E-507.

<!-- issue:1011 -->**Q: Kiosk touch not working?**
A: Wipe the screen first (seriously, grease). Then reboot it and check the touch driver is connected. Calibration is in the maintenance menu on the kiosk.
