# New content not showing, old content still playing

## Summary

Content changes made in the console have not reached the player, usually because the playlist is assigned to the wrong device or group, the schedule dates are off, or the player has not synced since the change.

## Before you start

- Device ID or device group name
- Name of the playlist that should be playing

## Steps

1. Confirm the new playlist is assigned to the correct device or device group.
2. Check the schedule start and end dates and the site's time zone.
3. Confirm the player has synced since the change (last sync time in the console).
4. Trigger a manual sync from the console.

## Escalate if

- Player has synced but still plays old content.
